How to play

Sol←→ move · ↑ jump
LunaAD move · W jump

Online, either set of keys controls your own character. Gamepads work too. In an online room, click Sol or Luna to play as them, or swap from the pause menu.

  • Every level needs both of you. What one of you does opens the way for the other.
  • Sun pools are safe for Sol and deadly for Luna.
  • Moon pools are safe for Luna and deadly for Sol.
  • Shade pools swallow anyone.
  • Pressure plates work while someone (or a crate) stands on them. Levers stay where you push them.
  • Collect your gems, then both stand at your own doors to finish.
  • Esc pause · R restart
