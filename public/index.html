How to play

Sol←→ move · ↑ jump
LunaAD move · W jump

Online, either set of keys controls your own character. Gamepads work too. In an online room, click Sol or Luna to play as them, or swap from the pause menu. Press Enter or the chat button at the top right to talk to your partner.

  • Every level needs both of you. What one of you does opens the way for the other.
  • Sun pools are safe for Sol and deadly for Luna.
  • Moon pools are safe for Luna and deadly for Sol.
  • Shade pools swallow anyone.
  • Pressure plates work while someone (or a crate) stands on them. Levers stay where you push them.
  • Collect your gems, then both stand at your own doors to finish.
  • Dodgeball (versus): hold ↓ (Sol) or S (Luna) to aim, release to throw. A tap throws straight; holding swings the aim up and down. Hit the other side before the ball touches the floor to score, and jump or hide to dodge. A missed ball belongs to whoever's side it stops on; hold it over 6 seconds and it goes to the other side. First to 5 hits wins.
  • Esc pause · R restart
  • Runner (versus): the view scrolls on its own and keeps speeding up. Stay ahead of the dusk at the left edge, jump the pits, thorns and pools, ride the chain planks and lanterns. The last one standing wins.
